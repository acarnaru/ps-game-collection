General Notes

Online Only

  • Requires a constant Internet connection (also known as live-service), for both single and multiplayer content, including progression and achievemnts.
  • If a stable Internet connection is not possible, or the servers are offline, the game is technically unplayable.

Back to Catalogue

Discontinued

  • The official servers have been shut down by the developers / host, therefore rendering the game unplayable.
  • Even though the game can still be installed and booted, no progression can be made past the main menu.

No Trophies

  • Even though the collection focuses only on trophy-enabled games, certain older PS3 titles, usually a part of bundles or collections, do not have trophy support.

3D Compatible

  • Supports Stereoscopic 3D technology, which also requires a Stereoscopic 3D enabled TV / monitor.
  • Can also be played on regular TVs / monitors if a Stereoscopic 3D one is unavailable.
